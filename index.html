MEET PRISCILA FUENTES

As an authentic Brazilian dance instructor, performer, and choreographer based in Minnesota, Priscila is dedicated to preserving and celebrating the joy, complexity, and liberating expression of Samba.

Latin Dance MN serves as a vibrant cultural bridge, connecting dancers of all backgrounds, levels, and ages to the syncopated rhythms of Brazil. From fundamental footwork to spectacular carnival staging, Priscila provides supportive, high-energy coaching that empowers you to dance with freedom, confidence, and authentic style.

Pure Energy

High-tempo cardio, rich rhythmic expression, and sheer joy.

Cultural Heritage

Rooted in Afro-Brazilian traditions, history, and community storytelling.

Confidence Built

A highly supportive space to unlock body movement and self-expression.

All Levels Welcome

Carefully structured steps for complete beginners to professional performers.

PAST SHOW SPOTLIGHT

AURORA DO SAMBA

The Dawn of Brazilian Rhythm in Minnesota

Aurora do Samba was a unique and magical multicultural show presented by Latin Dance MN. Audiences experienced a majestic journey through Brazilian history, folklore, and carnival celebration, featuring mesmerizing choreographies, live traditional percussion, capoeira performances, and elaborate, authentic feathered costumes flown straight from Rio.

This show was a truly unique and magical experience. We are currently looking for sponsors and partners to help us bring this spectacular performance back to the stage! If you're interested in sponsoring the next show, please contact us.
